Upgrades are permanent and kick in automatically every time you grab that power-up on the track.

    How to play

    Keyboard

    • ← → or A D — switch lanes
    • ↑ W Space — jump
    • ↓ S — roll (drops you fast in mid-air)
    • E or Shift — hoverboard
    • P or Esc — pause

    Touch

    • Swipe left / right / up / down
    • Double-tap for a hoverboard

    Tips

    • Jump the red & white hurdles, roll under the yellow “DUCK!” gates.
    • Ramps lead up onto train roofs — the safest place to be.
    • Trains with glowing headlights are coming at you.
    • Clip a train or hurdle from the side and you stumble. Stumble twice while the warden is close and you're caught!
    • A hoverboard absorbs one crash.
